IIT JAM Mathematics
Joint Admission Test for MSc
Exam Date: February (Annually)
Eligibility: BSc with Mathematics for 2+ years
Key Topics: Real Analysis, Linear Algebra, Abstract Algebra, Complex Analysis
Career Options: MSc at IITs/NITs, Integrated PhD, Research
Best for BSc High Success Rate
🎯
GATE Mathematics
Graduate Aptitude Test
Exam Date: February (Annually)
Eligibility: BSc Mathematics (Limited MTech options)
Key Topics: All JAM topics + PDE, Numerical Analysis, Topology
Career Options: PSUs, PhD with fellowship, Limited MTech
Higher Difficulty PSU Jobs

  • ✅ Join online math communities

MSc → MTech Route
Sequential Progression

Since direct MTech from BSc is limited, consider:

  1. Complete MSc from IIT via JAM
  2. Maintain high CGPA (8.5+)
  3. Apply for MTech or Integrated PhD
  4. Alternative: NITs accepting BSc+GATE
