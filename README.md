# 📚 BSc Mathematics Career Roadmap

A comprehensive career guidance platform for BSc Mathematics students, specifically designed for 2nd-year IGNOU students targeting multiple career paths including IIT JAM, GATE, CAT, UPSC, and international opportunities.

🌐 **Live Demo**: [here](https://algoguy09.github.io/gg/)

## 📋 Features

- **Comprehensive Exam Information**: Detailed guides for IIT JAM, GATE, CAT, UPSC, SSC CGL
- **Career Path Analysis**: Multiple career trajectories with success probability analysis
- **80/20 Study Plan**: Optimized preparation strategy focusing on overlapping subjects
- **Resource Library**: Curated free and paid resources for each exam
- **Interactive Tools**: Study plan generator, score calculator, Notion export
- **Daily Schedule**: Hour-by-hour timetable for maximum efficiency
- **Timeline Roadmap**: Year-wise milestones from 2nd year BSc to career launch

## 🎯 Target Audience

- 2nd & 3rd year BSc Mathematics students
- IGNOU and other university students
- Students preparing for multiple competitive exams
- Career switchers considering MBA or government services
